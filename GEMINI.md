## Agent Guidelines & Ground Rules

1. **Source of Truth:** Consult [`docs/LORE_CANON.md`](./docs/LORE_CANON.md) before implementing or modifying any environments, characters, enemies, stages, worldbuilding, visuals, dialogue, or story elements.
2. **Current Prototype Context:** The active playable build represents **Main Campaign Stage 1 (The Thawing Crash-Site Crater)**—the actual beginning of Endo Nine where the ship has crashed near the first Overseer artifact and the frozen planet is freshly thawing (blended thawed soil, frost/snow patches, crater rock, and reawakening alien flora). The Dimensional Loop remains canonical as the later alternate route at Stage 8.
3. **Core Revelation:** The Overseers mistook the aliens' rapid biological reproduction and planetary cultivation for military preparations for war.
4. **Three Established Endings:**
   - **Bad:** Alien victory and galactic expansion against the Overseers.
   - **Normal:** Planet refrozen; human survival with unresolved moral suppression of the alien species.
   - **Good:** Reconciliation and treaty between the Overseer and the aliens following the loop's revelation.
5. **Data & Code Scope:** This narrative is development context. Do **NOT** insert it into Supabase or PostgreSQL schemas. Do not modify gameplay code or rewrite established canon as part of lore tasks.
